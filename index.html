Elias' milk

Elias has relied on 100% breast milk since the day he was born.

In the beginning, the bulk of his milk came from Kristen, a donor in Missouri. More recently, the majority has come from donors in New York City.

Every few days, one of us goes out to pick up breast milk. Sometimes the amount is small, as little as 60 ounces, but sometimes it can be as much as 500 ounces. We have traveled to Queens, Bay Ridge, the Upper East Side, Bed-Stuy, Fort Greene, and Park Slope.

On average, Elias consumes over 30 ounces per day. He has received milk from 12 different donors in 9 neighborhoods across 2 states and 3 boroughs. We've now gone out 23 times, cumulatively traveled over 174 miles, and spent 22.7 hours in transit to pick up his milk.

Elias is now 8.5 months old and weighs over 20 lbs. We have at least another 2 months' worth of milk in our freezers and hope to make it to one year.

We are eternally grateful to everyone who has donated their milk.
